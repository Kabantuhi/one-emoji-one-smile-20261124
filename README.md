## Why this emoji

The judge picks the emoji most likely to make a stranger smile: warm at first glance, universal, and chosen rather than default. A hug is the warmest single symbol that is not the first smiley that comes to mind — kind, friendly, readable by any stranger, with no irony and no intimacy.
